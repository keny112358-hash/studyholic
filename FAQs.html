MCAT Frequently Asked Questions

What can I expect when taking the MCAT?

You will be provided a center-provided noteboard and marker, center-provided storage key, center-provided foam, and wireless earplugs. There will be breaks in-between each section, where upon completion, you must notify a proctor to be excused to begin your break. You are not permitted to access any electronic device during breaks. Your phone will be sealed in a plastic bag, and you will not be permitted to break it open until the end of the exam.

What should I bring on test day?

ID, registration confirmation, and a sweater. Please note that jackets, snacks, beverages, study materials, or electronics are not permitted in the testing area and must be kept in break areas or lockers.

Are there limits on retaking the MCAT?

Maximum of 7 times in your lifetime. Plan attempts strategically.

How long should I study for the MCAT?

Study time ranges from 3 months to a year. Use official AAMC tests timed to analyze errors and simulate test day. Focus on understanding mistakes and areas which you need to review.

What if an unexpected event occurs during test day?

Please plan to be in the testing center 30 minutes before your scheduled time. If an unexpected event occurs during your exam, you are permitted to cancel your score following its completion. Note that all cancelled scores will still show up on your record, and that you will not be able to know your score if you requested a score cancellation.
